Core5 Academy

Enterprise Learning Management System (LMS & EdTech Suite)

Home → Core5 Academy

Core5 Academy is a scalable, cloud-native Learning Management System (LMS) engineered to transform traditional education into immersive digital learning experiences. Built for higher-education universities, K-12 school networks, coaching institutes, and enterprise corporations, Core5 Academy seamlessly bridges instructors, learners, and administrators on one intuitive ecosystem.

The platform automates the entire learning lifecycle: from dynamic curriculum authoring and interactive live virtual classrooms to automated assessment evaluation, anti-cheat AI proctoring, and blockchain-verifiable digital credential issuance.

Target Sectors & Organizations: Universities, engineering & medical colleges, test prep institutes, vocational academies, and corporate enterprises conducting employee onboarding, compliance certification, and continuous upskilling.
Key Modules & Capabilities of Core5 Academy
  1. 1
    Interactive Virtual Classrooms: Built-in HD live video conferencing with digital whiteboard collaboration, real-time student doubt-clearing, live polls, breakout room discussions, and automated high-efficiency lecture recording archives.
  2. 2
    Drag-and-Drop Course Curriculum Builder: Create rich multi-format syllabi supporting interactive SCORM packages, streaming video modules, downloadable PDFs, podcasts, coding labs, and tiered prerequisites with ease.
  3. 3
    AI Proctoring & Auto-Evaluated Assessments: Conduct high-stakes examinations safely with browser lockdown, webcam anti-cheat face tracking, randomized question banks, instant objective auto-grading, and subjective rubric evaluation.
  4. 4
    Deep Learner Analytics & Drop-off Insights: Track granular student engagement, attendance percentages, video watch duration, assignment submission rates, and automated early warning alerts for at-risk learners.
  5. 5
    Automated Verifiable Certification: Generate custom-branded, tamper-proof certificates with unique verification QR codes, expiration date tracking for corporate compliance, and 1-click LinkedIn profile sharing.
  6. 6
    Multi-Tenant Institutional Governance: Manage multiple campuses, colleges, departments, faculty rosters, and fee payment gateways under a centralized master administrative dashboard with role-based access.
