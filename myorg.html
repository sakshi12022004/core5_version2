MyOrg

Google Enterprise & Education Plus Activity Monitor & Governance Console

Home → MyOrg

MyOrg is an advanced governance, user activity auditing, and domain security console designed specifically for educational institutions, universities, and enterprise organizations operating Google Workspace Enterprise and Google Workspace for Education Plus.

While native Google Admin consoles provide foundational controls, managing thousands of active faculty, student, or employee accounts across complex Organizational Units (OUs) often leads to data leakages, neglected inactive licenses, and unmonitored external file sharing. MyOrg gives IT administrators deep telemetric visibility, actionable DLP security alerts, and automated license reclaim workflows.

Target Sectors & Administrators: Universities, large K-12 school districts, enterprise corporations, Google Workspace Super Admins, Chief Information Security Officers (CISOs), and IT infrastructure directors.
Comprehensive Security & Monitoring Capabilities of MyOrg
  1. 1
    Real-Time User Engagement & Telemetry Auditing: Live heatmaps and granular activity logs tracking student and staff adoption across Google Classroom, Google Meet, Drive, Docs, and Gmail with trend indicators.
  2. 2
    Data Loss Prevention (DLP) & External Sharing Alerts: Instant detection when sensitive institutional documents, student records, or internal confidential files are shared with external personal email domains or set to public links.
  3. 3
    License Optimization & Substantial Cost Reclaim: Identify dormant, suspended, or under-utilized Education Plus and Enterprise licenses automatically. Reclaim and reassign them before costly annual renewal commitments.
  4. 4
    Simplified Multi-OU Hierarchical Management: Perform bulk user provisioning, policy enforcement, organizational unit transfers, and password resets through an interface engineered to be 10x faster than native consoles.
  5. 5
    Google Meet Safety & Automated Attendance Tracking: Detailed logs of meeting durations, participant join/leave timestamps, external guest intrusion prevention, and automated student attendance compilation for faculty.
  6. 6
    Audit-Ready Compliance & Forensic Log Archiving: Export compliant security audit reports fulfilling statutory data privacy frameworks, cyber insurance requirements, and institutional accreditation standards.
