Medisys

Hospital Management Information System (HMIS / Healthcare ERP)

Home → Medisys

Medisys is an all-inclusive Hospital Management Information System (HMIS) specifically designed to unify healthcare administration, patient care, clinical workflows, and financial management into a single, high-reliability platform. Built to serve multi-specialty hospitals, nursing homes, and diagnostic clinical centers, Medisys removes paperwork bottlenecks, eliminates manual billing calculation discrepancies, and ensures real-time clinical data availability across all departments.

Whether coordinating emergency OPD admissions, allocating inpatient beds, dispensing medications through an integrated digital pharmacy, or processing cashless medical insurance claims, Medisys keeps every stakeholder synchronized with precision and zero latency.

Target Sectors & Facilities: Multi-specialty hospitals, healthcare chains, day-care surgical centers, superspecialty clinics, pathology labs, and diagnostic centers requiring full NABH and statutory healthcare reporting compliance.
End-to-End Capabilities & Clinical Modules of Medisys
  1. 1
    Outpatient (OPD) & Inpatient (IPD) Management: Seamless patient registration with barcode cards, intelligent digital token queue display, doctor slot scheduling, inpatient ward/bed allocation, and automated single-click discharge summaries.
  2. 2
    Digital Electronic Medical Records (EMR): Doctors can securely access historical clinical vitals, past diagnoses, digital prescriptions, lab test trends, and high-resolution diagnostic imaging within seconds from any hospital terminal or tablet.
  3. 3
    Integrated Pharmacy & Stock Inventory: Real-time batch tracking, automated near-expiry alerts, minimum inventory thresholds, automatic purchase requisition generation, and direct POS billing integration with inpatient tabs.
  4. 4
    Cashless Insurance (TPA) & Medical Billing: Comprehensive pre-authorization claim workflows, corporate empanelment tariffs, GST-compliant multi-tier invoicing, automatic deduction tracking, and real-time ledger accounting.
  5. 5
    Pathology & Radiology Lab Integration: Bidirectional interfacing with lab diagnostic analyzers, digital sample barcode tracking, pathology doctor digital signature authorization, and automated SMS/WhatsApp dispatch of test reports to patients.
  6. 6
    NABH Accreditation & Statutory Regulatory Compliance: Audit-ready clinical quality indicators, mortality registers, infection control tracking, and role-based zero-trust security controls protecting sensitive patient health records (EHR/PHR).
