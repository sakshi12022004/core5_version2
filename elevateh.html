ElevateH

AI Resume Parsing, Campus Recruitment & Student Career Suite

Home → ElevateH

ElevateH is an end-to-end AI campus placement and career guidance ecosystem connecting colleges, universities, Training & Placement Officers (TPOs), students, and corporate recruiters into one synchronized recruitment workflow.

Traditional campus hiring involves thousands of chaotic resumes, missed recruitment drives, and recruiter fatigue. ElevateH leverages advanced NLP resume parsing to extract student competencies, benchmark ATS scores, match candidates to corporate vacancies, and automate interview scheduling in a fraction of the time.

Target Sectors & Users: Engineering & MBA colleges, university placement cells, TPOs, corporate talent acquisition teams, and graduating students seeking high-value employment.
Comprehensive Features & Capabilities of ElevateH
  1. 1
    AI Resume Parsing & ATS Scoring: Scans multi-format resumes (PDF/DOCX), automatically indexes technical skills, academic achievements, and internships, and provides actionable recommendations to optimize ATS compatibility scores.
  2. 2
    Intelligent Job Matching & Skill Gap Analytics: Machine-learning algorithms match student profiles to corporate job requisitions while highlighting missing industry competencies and recommending targeted upskilling certifications.
  3. 3
    Unified TPO Placement Management Portal: Manage on-campus and pool-campus placement drives, verify student eligibility criteria, broadcast instant WhatsApp/email announcements, and share filtered candidate batches with recruiters in one click.
  4. 4
    Recruiter Candidate Screening Console: Corporate hiring teams can filter thousands of students instantly using custom filters for CGPA, coding assessment scores, technical stack, backlogs, and location preferences.
  5. 5
    Automated Interview Scheduling & Slot Booking: Direct calendar integration for recruiter panel availability, automated interview reminder pings to students, virtual meeting room provisioning, and live round-by-round status tracking.
  6. 6
    NIRF & NAAC Institutional Reporting: Generate 1-click audit-ready placement reports, CTC salary distribution curves, branch-wise hiring percentages, and longitudinal employment metrics for higher-education accreditations.
