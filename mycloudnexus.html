MyCloudNexus

Cloud FinOps, Governance & Multi-Cloud Cost Optimization Suite

Home → MyCloudNexus

MyCloudNexus is an intelligent FinOps intelligence and automated cost optimization platform built for tech startups, digital agencies, and large enterprises running multi-cloud workloads on Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and Microsoft Azure.

Modern cloud billing is notoriously convoluted and prone to surprise spikes. MyCloudNexus cuts through the complexity with continuous infrastructure discovery, predictive machine-learning cost anomalies, automated rightsizing recommendations, and unified multi-cloud billing ledgers, typically reducing monthly cloud spend by 20% to 40%.

Target Sectors & Teams: Startups, SaaS enterprises, CTOs, DevOps engineers, FinOps practitioners, and finance heads looking to gain absolute transparency and stop cloud billing leakage.
Core Capabilities & Architecture of MyCloudNexus
  1. 1
    Real-Time Unified Multi-Cloud Telemetry: Connect your AWS, GCP, and Azure accounts via read-only IAM roles in under five minutes. Aggregate all compute, network egress, storage, serverless, and database expenses onto a single live pane.
  2. 2
    Automated Idle Resource & Waste Elimination: Detect forgotten virtual machine instances, unattached EBS/disk volumes, unassociated elastic IP addresses, and dormant development clusters with 1-click automated cleanup routines.
  3. 3
    Machine-Learned Budget Spike & Anomaly Alerts: Proprietary algorithmic tracking alerts engineering teams in real time via Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Webhooks before unexpected runaway queries or runaway loops result in thousands of dollars in surprise invoices.
  4. 4
    Commitment & Reserved Instance Optimization: Get data-backed procurement recommendations for AWS Savings Plans, GCP Committed Use Discounts (CUD), and Azure Reservations to lock in up to 72% discounts without risky over-commitment.
  5. 5
    Granular Unit Economics & Chargeback Tagging: Allocate cloud costs accurately down to microservices, business divisions, client accounts, or git repositories with automated tag compliance governance.
  6. 6
    Non-Production Environment Auto-Scheduler: Automatically power down staging, QA, and development infrastructure during weekends and non-business hours, cutting staging costs by up to 65% instantly.
